# BQ27220 battery observations

The [portable gauge crate](../../crates/bq27220/src/lib.rs) reads battery observations through `embedded-hal` I2C.
`Bq27220` implements platform `BatteryGauge` without ESP peripherals or an executor.
It acquires [shared bus ownership](../platform/shared-bus.md) once per complete sample.
The low-level `battery` function requires an already acquired bus. Firmware supplies scheduling and publication.
A bus error discards partial measurements. `ChangedDuringRead` rejects a change in presence or readiness during measurement.

The chip fixes its seven-bit I2C address at `0x55`. Board wiring does not select this address.
The bus adds the direction bit, producing `0xAA` for writes and `0xAB` for reads.
See the [BQ27220 datasheet, section 7.3.1.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq27220.pdf).

The adapter reports the gauge's internal temperature. Interpreting it as cell temperature could give a misleading thermal reading.
Negative current indicates discharge. See the [register adapter](../../crates/bq27220/src/lib.rs) and the [BQ27220 reference manual](https://www.ti.com/lit/ug/sluubd4/sluubd4.pdf), table 2-1 and sections 2.6–2.28.

`BATTPRES` supplies reported battery presence.
The fitted cell and gauge configuration determine the accuracy of this indication.
`INITCOMP`, `CFGUPDATE`, and `CALMD` determine measurement readiness.
The adapter omits measurements during startup, configuration, calibration, or reported battery absence.
It checks presence and readiness again after the measurements. A change rejects the complete sample.

The adapter reads existing capacity and health estimates without changes to the cell profile.
It does not unseal, calibrate, reset, or configure the gauge.
The backend marks estimates as unverified because software has not established the fitted cell parameters or calibration.
Gauge readiness alone cannot verify those conditions.

## Verification limits

Native [gauge tests](../../crates/bq27220/tests/observations.rs) verify units, signed values, readiness, invalid measurements, battery removal, and failures at each bus transaction.
The simulator retains a separate test for independent gauge and charger failures.
These checks do not establish physical access, cell calibration, or measurement accuracy.
